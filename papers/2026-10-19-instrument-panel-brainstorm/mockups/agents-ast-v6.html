Subagents v6: AST flight

Floor (x,y) = a coarse AST of the code: the root nearest you, then directories, files, and classes and functions growing away into the distance.

Agents launch from the spawn point (upper left), then fly between nodes. Each move is an arc above the floor that lands on the next node, and longer jumps arc higher. The latest hop is bright; older hops fade (the last 5 are kept). Moves come every 5–12 s..

Left column: last action, node and tokens per agent. A red ring means two agents are in the same file and one is editing. Fake data.
