## Questions the design must answer

1. What runs concurrently, who owns each VM/child, and which execution remains
   available when an operator turn returns or the terminal disconnects?
2. What survives controller/process failure, and what becomes uncertain? How do
   stable decisions, attempts, child outcomes and segment state commit together?
3. How do joins/races, nested resource limits, cancellation, detached work and
   unknown remote effects interact without false completion or deadlock?
4. How can models author and change programs, contexts, execution graphs,
   participants and experiments during ordinary work with coherent activation?
5. How do events, durable waits/schedules, dataflow/statecharts and artifact reuse
   share machinery without erasing their distinct semantics?
6. How do external providers, services, SSH executors and future E2EE peers fit
   without Blackbird governing shared computation or inventing exactly-once effects?
7. What actual hot-path work, copies, wakeups, I/O and recovery complexity must be
   measured, and which direct oracle proves each promised behavior?
