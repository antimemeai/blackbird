# One conceptual challenge and its resolution

2026-10-15. The HCI researcher challenged the written integrated theoretical core
once. Its [independent report](hci/INTEGRATED_CHALLENGE.md) is retained unchanged.
Root integrated the following two refinements into
[INTERACTION_THEORY.md](../../docs/INTERACTION_THEORY.md).

| Finding | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Words such as appropriate, relevant and useful could rescue a causal explanation after the outcome is known. | The derivation section now requires naming the episode, alternatives, expected mechanism and offsetting costs before comparing. It explicitly says to reject or narrow a local claim when the predicted mechanism fails or worse costs dominate, without retrospectively redefining the intention. |
| Familiar-task comparisons alone could favor a narrower interface and miss the operator's generative agency. | The theory now asks which useful intentions and methods become possible or disappear, and whether participants can invent, understand and revise their instruments. This appears in both the comparison discipline and the discriminating-question table. |

The reviewer found that the core supplied explanatory mechanisms rather than
merely renaming feature wishes, and preserved delegation, expression, beauty and
human/model distinctions. That judgment does not establish the truth of the
causal predictions or qualify an implementation.

The SR-71 controls and saturation studies were developed concurrently and added
as a source-grounded case and refinement. Their owning researchers verified their
source/version/page scopes. The conceptual challenge is not represented as a new
review of those reports or validation of aircraft measurements. No review of
reviews, third assurance layer, product benchmark or evaluator campaign follows.
