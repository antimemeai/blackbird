# SR-71 controls: acquisition limits

2026-10-15. Required bounded control/cockpit/procedure material was acquired from the original-document mirrors listed in [sources.tsv](sources.tsv). No purchase or user action is required to use the current report.

- Preferred scan index [SR-71.org manual](https://www.sr-71.org/blackbird/manual/): HTTP503 from web access and `curl --fail --location` on this date. FirebirdV8 original-document PDFs supplied the studied controls/procedures instead.
- Exact dated edition of Handbook II: a 151-page original scan was acquired from USAF SIG; its title and relevant pages were visually inspected. The scanned front matter does not establish the date. NASA cites a 1995 publication, but the report does not silently assign that edition to this scan. A dated title/revision page or official matching copy would improve provenance if further aircraft engineering depends on this handbook.
- Full flight-manual distribution: only Parts 1 and 2 were acquired, 248 image pages each. [Part 3](https://www.firebirdv8.com/sr/sr71_fm_pt3.pdf) remains optional for later navigation/mission-system scope. The manual's 1052-page effective list is not a claim that this mirrored distribution includes every original page.
- Firsthand pilot accounts and task saturation: the independent workload lane studies NASA's handling-quality evaluation and workload literature. Popular speed-check stories, flight-simulator manuals and commercial summaries are not used here as technical evidence.

No aircraft operating procedure, certification, numerical workload threshold or simulator validation is needed for the conditional Blackbird interaction claims in this report.
