- Original failed composition sync stack was never sampled. Kernel versus scheduling remains unknown; do not rerun its source hook or diagnose storage under this study's authority.
- Multi-page/retired-resident/generic-tool original inspection; real scroll-selection-copy-return under live output; blocked terminal writer/control ordering; GUI modified Enter/ESC ambiguity, IME/keyboard layouts, screen reader and actual font/contrast/motion profiles.

Where unavailable, the design must show an honest unavailable/unknown state and retain original references. It must not fill the gap with guessed delivery, safe replay, completed termination or invented usability evidence.
